How these deals were checked

Every deal on this page was found and price-checked by hand on 27 November 2026 (Black Friday) — the first 34 around 14:05 WIB, the next 6 around 17:20 WIB and the next 20 around 21:30 WIB — and the newest 12 on 28 November 2026 around 09:45 WIB, each carrying its own check time on its card. Each price was verified in one of two ways, and every card says which: against the store's own Black Friday release (Best Buy's release and Newegg's release via BusinessWire), or against a named deals roundup that links the store (Gizmodo, 9to5Toys, PopSci, ComicBook, Yahoo, Benzinga, TheStreet, MacObserver, Product Bible, Kicks & Grips, House Cultures, RetailShout, Yellos, McGraw Media, Clark Deals, SlashGear, SVR Deals, Top10-Best, PC Guide). Amazon's product pages refuse automated reads (HTTP 403 when we tried on 27 Nov), so every Amazon price here is confirmed by two or more independent roundups, never one — and the AirPods Pro 3 card links straight to the Amazon product page. Prices can change or sell out; the store link takes you to the product at that store so you can confirm the price before you buy. Discounts are computed from the sale price against the usual (list) price shown by the store, and every was-price was checked against its source — none was inflated. Where the same product is cheaper (or the same price) at another store, the comparison strip below the grid says so, and the deal's own detail page shows the other store's price. The verdict on each card is our honest read, with a one-sentence reason on the card: Hot deal is 40% off or more, Good deal is 20–39% off, Mild deal is under 20% off — a small saving, flagged as such. The deal score is discount% + 15 for a genuine new/all-time low + 10 for saving $200 or more + 10 more for saving $500 or more, capped at 100.
